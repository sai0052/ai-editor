Silence & Filler-Word Remover

Upload a video or audio file. It'll transcribe it, cut the dead air and "um"s, and hand back a tighter cut.

What this tool does

Recording a video or podcast usually means dealing with long pauses, dead air, and filler words like "um," "uh," and "like." Editing those out by hand is slow. This tool uses AI speech recognition to automatically find and remove them, handing you back a tighter, more polished cut without any manual timeline editing.

How it works

  1. Upload a video or audio file.
  2. The AI transcribes your speech and detects filler words and long silences.
  3. Those sections are automatically cut out and the remaining clips are stitched back together.
  4. Download your edited file, ready to share or publish.

Who it's for

  • YouTubers and podcasters who want faster turnaround on raw recordings.
  • Course creators and educators tightening up lecture or tutorial footage.
  • Anyone who records screen recordings, voiceovers, or interviews and wants a cleaner first pass before final editing.
